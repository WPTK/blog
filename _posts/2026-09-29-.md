# Why?

~~*During these unprecedented times...*~~

~~*Now more than ever...*~~

I haven't written "creatively" in a while (air quotes because I'm not sure if a blog counts as creative). I'm sort of caught in an ideological bind. Basically, there are two wolves inside me: 

Wolf A) *AI is not the end-all, be-all to advancing human society. Go touch grass.*

Wolf B) *Do you really want to be the punchcard operator ranting about how minicomputers are a fad and smugly explain why everyone needs punchcards?*
