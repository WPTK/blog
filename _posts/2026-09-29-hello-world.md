First post. To publish a new one, add a file to `_posts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` with a `title` and `date` at the top, then commit it.

Optional front matter:

- `description`: used for the page summary in search results and link previews
- `tags`: a list, shown at the bottom of the post
