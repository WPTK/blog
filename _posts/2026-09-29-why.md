~~*Now more than ever...*~~

I haven't written "creatively" in a while (air quotes because I'm not sure if a blog counts as creative). I'm sort of caught in an ideological bind. Basically, there are two wolves inside me: 

Wolf A) *AI is not the end-all, be-all to advancing human society. Go touch grass.*

Wolf B) *Do you really want to be the punchcard operator ranting about how minicomputers are a fad and smugly explain why everyone needs punchcards?* 

This blog is that struggle. 

# What will be AI slop?

I'll try to remember disclose. Controversial opinion: Not disclosing AI usage is kind of silly. 

I can tell you off the bat, I found a Jekyll layout and straight up told Claude to copy it. 

My original idea that spurred all of this, [Kipple](https://kipple.cc/) is entirely coded in Claude. I knew what I wanted, what I like from other apps, and tried my best to be a good project/product manager. I'm certainly not a programmer. 

# I'm Sorry, Did You Say Kipple?

I did say Kipple. If you know your PKD/pulp-sci-fi, Kipple is the perfect word for an RSS reader I'd use, because every time I look away, 100 more stories are posted. 

I want to blog my experience with creating AI apps. I am also going to have Claude "join in" on some of the conversations. I am having Claude preserve our conversations, "meetings", milestones, timelines, summaries, etc. It doesn't know it yet, but it will be writing it's own version of this story as well. 

# So what are you going to write about? Why do you use title case incorrectly?

I got tired of hitting shift. 

This blog should cover all of my interests, of which I have varying levels of understanding and involvement with: 

- Tech
- Books (I did invent my own rating system, the C3L system, go check it out back on my website) 
- Fonts/Type
- Research as a concept

If you're looking for hot political takes, this ain't the place. I'm not apolitical, this just isn't the place. I want the internet to be 1990s fun, not 2010s+ shitty.
