I wonder if at some point, as a child, I looked up at the sky, and saw something super cool fly over and not even realize it.

I didn't get into aviation until I was an adult. I didn't really have a *fear* of flying per-se, but I wasn't a fan. And for the first half of my career, I traveled for at least one full quarter per year for 5 years. That got me over the fear pretty quick.

As I kid, I have vivid memories of [ValuJet Flight 592](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ValuJet_Flight_592]. At least, I think it's a memory. [There were considerably more plane crashes in the 90s](my.ntsb.gov/monthly), so it's probably a false one. Who knows.

Now, as a certifiable AvGeek (I've got ADSB, ACARS, VDLM2 receivers in my attic, to start), I look back at that time period of my childhood and think about stuff that was still flying, like the [L-1011 Tristar](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/history/l-1011.html).

I'll have to do some research, see if I can find some old KATL/KJAX/KMCO timetables and try to do some digging there. Or spend too much time on AirFleets (more than I already do)
